## Why Pass 1 was not approved

- A visible magenta/purple edge fringe remained around the vehicle.
- Gold was too dominant on the wheels and window surround.
- The headlamp graphic did not read consistently as the immutable Twin-Blade DRL.
- The center mark read as a loose winged V rather than a compact Winged-V Shield.
- The overall face was closer to a generic tuned luxury sedan than a locked VROO flagship.

## Pass 3 improvements

- Wide, low hexagonal grille and dense dark mesh retained.
- Compact Winged-V Shield emblem established at the grille center.
- Near-side Twin-Blade DRL reads as two separated cool-white blades.
- Gold reduced to thin premium accents on the grille, lower lip, side skirt and wheel rim.
- Wheels changed to dark satin multi-spoke construction.
- Glass reflections neutralized; green chroma contamination removed.
- Executive lower fascia retained without a deep race splitter.
