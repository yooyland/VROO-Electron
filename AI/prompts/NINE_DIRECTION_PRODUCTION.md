# Nine-Direction Production Rule

Do not generate a contact sheet. Generate each direction as a separate 2048×2048 transparent image.

Use the approved `front_45` master as the immutable identity reference.

Required files:
- front.png
- front_45.png
- right.png
- rear_right.png
- rear.png
- rear_left.png
- left.png
- front_left.png
- front_right.png

For every output:
- one vehicle only
- same canvas and vehicle scale
- same wheelbase, ride height and body kit
- same wheel design and gold trim
- same lamp and grille identity
- no text, environment, floor, shadow or reflection
